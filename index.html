How to use

A whole shift, in two screens

No training day. Open your line, fill the hour you're on, hit save — the sheet has it before the phone is back in your pocket.

🧮

No maths on the floor

LOR%, running totals and what's left to plan are worked out as you type. Nobody carries a calculator.

⏱️

Hour by hour

Ahmad takes 8AM, Karim takes 10AM. Empty slots stay open; filled ones show who entered them and when.

✏️

Fix a count, not the row

Miscounted the scrap? Change the quantity and the good pieces move straight back into output.

🔒

The plan stays put

Once the target is in, it's locked — so the plan can never quietly drift to match the result.
